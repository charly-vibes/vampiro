Implement only approved OpenSpec changes. Establish the Rust CLI contracts, then stabilize the CIR/plugin contracts. The first Rust frontend CIR milestone unlocks core seam-analysis E2E work; scan workflows follow the core finding contract. The Rust runner-input milestone unlocks law verification, which owns the first registered Rust law runner and need not wait for scan. Lifecycle analysis consumes stable CIR, core-finding, law-evidence, and Rust lifecycle-extraction contracts. Python, Clojure, and Julia extraction may begin independently after CIR stabilization, with final acceptance against stable core, law, and lifecycle contracts. Do not begin a change before its proposal and required contract milestones are approved.
